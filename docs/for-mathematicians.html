The proof route

The proof is not presented as a log-concavity proof. It uses geometric recursion, tree/forest synchronization, and a finite certificate bridge.

The Problem

Erdős Problem 993 asks for unimodality of the independent-set sequence of a finite tree. The public Lean statement is phrased for finite acyclic simple graphs, covering finite trees and forests.

Why Not Log-Concavity?

A natural first route is to prove log-concavity of the independent-set sequence. That route fails in the needed generality, so the project moves to structural geometry rather than treating log-concavity as the invariant.

Trees, Forests, And Synchronization

Trees and forests have related but distinct recursion laws. Products over forest components create a different coordinate system, so the proof needs a synchronization layer between the tree and forest views.

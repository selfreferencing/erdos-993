Public Theorem Surface

The external theorem is the finite acyclic simple graph statement. The internal SemanticTree theorem is proof machinery, not the public claim.

What This Is

The project combines ordinary Lean proof engineering, generated finite certificates, computational audits, and multi-agent AI proof search. The intended release standard is conventional: checked Lean source, reproducible commands, explicit trust boundaries, and outside review.

For mathematicians

The proof route moves beyond a failed log-concavity strategy into a geometric recursion analysis of trees and forests, including a synchronization layer between their different coordinate systems.

For Lean reviewers

The key review boundary is whether the P116 semantic actualization layer non-circularly connects actual selected packages to the generated finite certificate layer.
